About PilipiKnow

Empowering young Filipino learners through interactive and engaging digital flashcard materials.

PilipiKnow is an educational digital flashcard set designed to spark curiosity, encourage participation, and build confidence among young learners. The project focuses on creating visually engaging flashcards to support the English literacy development of first-grade Filipino learners. Its main objective is to examine how these flashcards influence students’ vocabulary acquisition and comprehension.

📖
👁️

Our Vision

To be a trusted learning companion for Filipino children, where every first-grade learner builds confidence in English and discovers the joy of learning through curiosity and play.

🎯

Our Mission

To create visually engaging digital flashcards that spark curiosity, encourage participation, and strengthen the vocabulary and comprehension of first-grade Filipino learners, while studying how these materials support their English literacy development.
